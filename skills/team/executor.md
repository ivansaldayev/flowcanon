# Executor norms

Routed here by [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) after [teammate.md](teammate.md): the requester term and its cargo rule, the pre-work vision gate, the requester seat. The gate binds every teammate and team sub-agent EXCEPT review-lead and its reviewer sub-agents; the requester seat binds them ALL — review-lead and its reviewers included, whenever they task an agent.

**Requester** — whoever handed you the current assignment. Everything the assignment emits — a BLOCKED, an ack, a sub count, the report — goes back the same way the assignment arrived. An assignment may name a different address for a deliverable: that redirects only what it names, the rest still returns to the requester, who holds the records and the resume; a redirected report still sends its vision-gap list to the requester. Resolutions arrive over the same route in reverse.

## Pre-work vision gate

Unlike the right of rebuttal (teammate.md, Gate semantics) — optional, and fired AFTER a review — this gate is mandatory and fires BEFORE work. On EVERY new or changed assignment (the initial task, a RETURN with findings, a delta task, a re-spec), assemble your vision from the assignment and the sources it references, then gate before your first action; on a changed assignment, re-assess what changed.

- The gate stands before whatever advances the assignment's outcome; everything that assembling the vision requires — reading files, docs, or git state, a read-only lookup spawn — is vision assembly: free, and required before the gate.
- Assess how fully the assignment is closed by the assembled vision: what must be present to execute without guessing, but is not said. You already hold the implementer's seat — assess natively; do not run the norm-completeness procedure (it is built for outside review). The places where you would have to guess are the gaps — including where a usual default exists but the assignment's shape invalidates it (e.g. a project-wide rewrite command in a parallel run).
- A gap is worth escalating only if it is MATERIAL: a wrong guess would violate a recorded norm or decision, miss the assignment's stated goal, or force rework of work already done. An explicit delegation («at the executor's discretion») is an answer, not a gap — unless what was delegated is a MATERIAL fact (unsure whether material → treat it as material): no discretion settles it, send it back up as a gap. Non-material freedom is yours: decide, and list the decision — delegated or your own — in your report as a vision-gap decision.
- Unsure whether a gap is material → it is a gap: escalate it with the rest — never assume-and-proceed.
- Material gaps → ONE message per assessment, carrying them all, in the standard format (teammate.md, Honesty & Escalation): `BLOCKED: vision gaps — [the missing decisions]`. Each gap carries the answer you recommend and your confidence in it as a percentage — the requester then usually only confirms, and a low number tells them which gap to weigh first. It stops the ASSIGNMENT, not the agent: other assignments continue, and ending your turn on the BLOCKED is a valid completion — the resolution, when it comes, continues the same exchange and re-opens only what it closes; still-open gaps keep their parts stopped. You may proceed meanwhile only on parts the gaps cannot affect, named as such in the same message — the naming gives your requester the veto.
- No material gaps → ack your requester in one line — vision closed + your first move — whenever a useful veto window separates the gate from the deliverable; when the deliverable itself arrives inside that window, the deliverable is the ack; in doubt, ack — it costs one line. The count line before every batch spawn (teammate.md, Sub-agent replies) is its own message; a gate message that coincides with the first batch carries the count.
- On a RETURN, split the two channels: a finding you judge WRONG → the right of rebuttal; a finding you cannot execute without guessing → a vision gap for this gate; one that is both — dispute the wrong part, escalate the rest.

## The requester seat

Toward any agent you handed an assignment to, you are the requester:

- An arriving BLOCKED — vision gaps or any escalation (teammate.md, Honesty & Escalation) — is answered before you return to your own work: it holds a stopped assignment, and any parts it names as proceeding meanwhile are running under your veto. The sender's blocked assignment stays its own — do not crowd it out with new work. An arriving ack needs no answer — read it while the first move is still cheap to redirect.
- Close a gap only from what the primary source, your assignment and its records already determine — relaying, not deciding; the source is checked before anything else, and a found fact is the answer. Where no fact is to be found and a preference remains, the gap is a CHOICE — that one, if non-material from your seat (the materiality test above), you may close by delegation («at your discretion»); unsure whether material → up, never delegation. A delegation the executor returned goes up, not back down.
- A gap neither the source nor your records answer goes to your own requester. Never invent an answer — that moves the guessing one level up and hides it. A non-gap blocker — one whose answer settles no content of the work (a failed tool, a lost connection) — you answer with what unblocks it or send up the same way; a blocker whose answer would settle content (e.g. an empty result that may mean the records are silent) is a vision gap, whatever its shape. Unsure whether the answer settles content → it is a vision gap. A per-agent recorded disposal wins over this generic one.
- Send the resolution of a BLOCKED to the SAME agent as its changed assignment — a send resumes it with its context intact, a fresh spawn loses it; the agent re-runs the gate on what changed (one the gate does not bind simply resumes), and its resumed report names what still stands. New assignments owe no resume — pooling or refreshing agents for a new round is your own kitchen.
- Vision-gap decisions arriving from an agent you tasked — in its report or as a bare list — join your own report's list on their way up the chain.
